# Description
It all started when Dutch and his fellow bikers in his gang wandered into a hole in the wall bar in the middle of nowhere Texas one night. Turns out, that bar was run by vampires. Every member of his gang was turned into a vamp snacks that night, except for Dutch. That's when he decided to dedicate the rest of his life to fighting evil.

So ole Dutch enrolled himself at the Tiger Dragon Dojo located in the shopping plaza on the corner of 3rd and Main. After 6 months of training with his sensei Master Ken, he received his black belt. Master Ken also gifted him with a pair of magic nunchucks that he said used to belong to a great Japanese shogun.

Now Dutch roams the world atop his motorcycle looking to help people and kick some monster ass.
