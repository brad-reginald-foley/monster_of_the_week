The Expert
# Description
Jessica E. Thorn is an Expert. Trained in civil engineering Jessica thought she had landed a cushy gig with the City DWP, the Department of Water and Power. Unfortunately due to the vagaries of the local hiring system she actually ended up as the newest of a handful of overworked employees at the other DWP, the Department of Weird and Paranormal. 

Tucked into the original charter of the city the department has existed, underfunded and overworked since the early 1900s. From her cramped East Hollywood office wedged up against the 101 Freeway Jessica learned to permit the local poltergeists, coordinate strategic closings of Griffith Park to accommodate the pack of octogenarian West Hollywood lycanthropes and maintain the warding salt lines that keep the kelpies out of the storm drain system. 

15 years into her career, with no applicable skills outside of the department she's settled into a jaded day to day routine. Unflappable and armed with a vast quantity of red tape, an official badge, a high viz vest and her trusty cold iron knife she has accepted that there is a lot out there she doesn't understand, but it still needs to pay the permitting fees regardless.
