# Description

The CEO of Creature Comforts. He's a wizard who has been experimenting on himself, absorbing the powers of creatures for years, decades maybe. He is cadaverously thin, does not look human any more. His eyes change constantly, sometimes black, sometimes gold. His mouth seems too large, his ears mismatched and his mouth almost like a muzzle. His teeth might be sharp, his skin looks the color of the water you wash paintbrushes in

When he eats the devil's food cake he gets about one and a half human size, with red skin, vestigial wings, horns, sharp teeth and claws.
