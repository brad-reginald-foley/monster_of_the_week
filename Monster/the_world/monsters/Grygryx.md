# Description
Minon of the devil being tortured and consumed in [[Creature Comforts Distributing Inc]]. A small, flying demonlike creature with red skin and bulging eyes. Frantic that her master, "Merodach" is missing, and warning about pain, and cages and "The End" for everything.

She (mis?)interprets [[Biff McGee]]'s prophecy. Instead of "Ridding the world of evil" she calls him the Dark Rider and thinks he's "Riding the world of evil"

She will show up as a contingency, at "sunset" time (after  [[Creature Confections]] ) when Sammy Davis' tomb has been desecrated in order to guide the hunters to [[Creature Comforts Distributing Inc]]. 

She's honestly pretty cute, and good hearted, but a bit chaotic
