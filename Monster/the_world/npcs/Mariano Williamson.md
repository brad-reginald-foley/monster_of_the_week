Sometimes a Hunter. A Flake class. Has a call-in radio show called The Mariano Experience (you're not alone) on KRYP, Kryptid Radio. Mostly funded by an eccentric heiress.
# Description
He's been homeless for stretches of his life, living out of a car. Lanky. Worn suit jacket. Flyaway hair and wild eyes. Has a surprisingly apt talent for correctly finding and identifying paranormal activity.

His call-in radio show is must-listen for any SoCal supernatural investigator, it's music, interspersed with people talking about their weird encounters. Most of the callers are just schizophrenic or meth-addled ramblers. But more often than you'd expect, some of the stories check out.

* **Gossip** (motivation: to pass on rumours)
