# Description
A youngish guy. Human. Thickset, early thirties. Divorced. The son of [[Lilit Gragarian's House|Lilit Gragarian]], and the father of Mimi. He works with [[Los Profanas]]. He accidentally gave a bunch of kids fairy essence infused fairy floss at his daughters birthday.

He's kind of a minion/npc. You can probably do all the "witness" stuff with him, but he's not a good guy. He tried to pull back from the most egregiously criminal aspects of Los Profanas, but was in charge of liaising with[[Creature Comforts Distributing Inc]] to get infused edibles for their enterprises anyway.;
