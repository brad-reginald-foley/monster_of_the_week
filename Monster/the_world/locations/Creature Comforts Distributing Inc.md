# Description

A largish industrial warehouse complex in Vernon. This is where the Big Bad is located. 

## loading bay
You enter through a gate on Alcoa, there is an automatic gate with bars, and a small parking lot. When you get through the loading bay doors, there are stacks of prepared confections in boxes piled against the walls, including 
* minute-maid
* candy corn
* fairy floss sugar
* angel food cake
* cinnamon trolls

In the loading bay there are a couple of guards (troll minions) and a customer loading at truck (organized crime guy)

## food prep area
Through a door is the food prep area, very large, with large ovens and counters and mixers and a vat for candy. Clearly not a huge industrial operation (more bespoke) but there is space for more equipment. Anomalously, many of the surfaces are covered in arcane runes and casting symbols in a terrifying script that hurts your eyes. 

A devil, three times the size of a human, is hanging from chains, alive, his arm flayed. This is where Big Bad [[Vernon Comfort]] comes

Knives and other potential weapons. No windows 

Additional food: 
* devil's food cake
* green slime jello shots
## dungeon
Another door leads to what looks like a torture chamber. There are **two angels** chained up, a **unicorn**, a number of **fairies** a couple **trolls**, a **slime**, and a **minotaur**. All dirty and with horrifying amputations
