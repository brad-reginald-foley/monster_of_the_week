# Description
A super bougie bakery and confection shop in the swishest part of Rodeo Drive. All white stone and subtly green glass, glowing faintly.

Confections are shown on pedestals, with lighting, under glass. Labels are in gold tracery in the pedestals. No prices. There is a door in the back, solidly locked. Two bouncers, with grey skin, and a single flawless androgynous clerk.
