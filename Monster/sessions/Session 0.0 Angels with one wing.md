# story outline
## in brief

There is a mysterious disaster at a birthday party, as several children go floating into the air after eating cotton candy. The seemingly whimsical misadventure turns out to have a macabre cause.
## details

There is a catering and bakery company called the "Creature Comforts" that has a shop front in Beverly Hills and a factory in the warehouse district. They vivisect creatures, and put their essences into food that they sell to the uber rich, as well as criminal elements in the city. They are looking to expand their franchise, and to capture still more powerful creatures (maybe gods?)

* minions are humans that have eaten either 
	* minotaur (strength, speed) minute-maid. occasional "moo"
	* angel (flight) angel food cake, halo. weakness hell fire
	* troll (strength) cinnamon trolls. weakness fire
* the main monster has a devil aspect 
	* devil's food cake
	* size, strength horns, wings, teeth, hellfire
	* weaknesses divine weapons, holy anything
* other creatures that are made into food are
	* fairies (flight, glamour) fairy floss. weakness iron
	* unicorns (male stamina) candy corn
